## Description
In 1982, a backhoe pulled human skulls out of a small pond in Titusville, Florida. Police thought it was a crime
scene. It was a 7,000-year-old cemetery - and 91 of the skulls still held preserved human brains.

This is the story of Windover: how archaeologists drained a graveyard with 160 wells, why nothing rotted in the
peat, the woven cloth they were wrapped in, the boy who was carried for his whole life, and the mystery of why
these people buried their dead underwater.
